ROUND & TURN SEQUENCE

1. PLAYER PHASE

Players take turns clockwise starting with First Player.

  • On your turn: Perform exactly 2 Actions (or 1 Action + Pass, or Pass immediately).
  • Keep going around: Turns continue around the table until all players have Passed.
  • Passing: Discard any number of Action and/or Contamination cards. You take no further actions this round.
  • Oxygen Check: If passing in a Section with Inactive Life Support, you still lose 1 O₂.

2. EVENT PHASE

  1. 1) Escape: Any player in the Lander may choose to launch it now (if AA is cleared). All aboard escape.
  2. 2) Fire Damage: Every Character and Intruder in a Room with Fire loses 1 Health.
  3. 3) Intruder Attacks: Intruders in rooms with Characters attack (largest first, targeting active player order).
  4. 4) Event Card: Draw 1 Event card. Move Intruders by matching direction icons, then resolve Event text.
  5. 5) Bag Development: Draw 1 token from the Intruder Bag and resolve on the Intruder table.

3. CLEANUP PHASE

  1. 6) First Player Change: Pass Starting Player token clockwise (skip dead/escaped/hibernating).
  2. 7) Drawing Cards: All players draw up to 5 Action Cards. (Reshuffle discard if empty).
  3. 8) Time Advancement: Advance Round Marker 1 space. Resolve any token in the newly entered space immediately.

Roll the 10-sided Noise Die after every move (unless a card specifically waives it):

1, 2, 3, or 4
Numbered Corridor Check: Check every adjacent Corridor matching that number:
  • If it already contains Intruders: Largest moves into your Room.
  • If it has a Noise Marker: Remove marker → draw Intruder token → place in Corridor.
  • If it has NO noise and NO intruders: Place 1 Noise Marker there.

PRO TIPS

CRITICAL RULES MOST PLAYERS MISS

  • Shooting does NOT automatically spend ammo: Ammo is ONLY spent when the Shoot Die rolls the Ammo-loss icon. Burst, however, always spends 1 ammo.
  • Passing still costs Oxygen: If you are in a Section where Life Support is Inactive, passing your turn still drains 1 O₂. Don't linger in unpowered sections.
  • Contamination cards cannot pay costs: You cannot discard Contamination to pay for Basic Actions. You can only ditch them when you Pass.
  • Closed Doors block almost everything: You cannot move, burst, shoot, throw grenades, or command through closed doors. Only Noise markers ignore doors.
  • Opportunity attacks max out at 3: If you flee a room/corridor with a swarm of intruders, only the 3 largest get opportunity attacks before you move.
  • Lander Health Immunity: Characters sitting safely inside the Lander cannot lose Health from outside effects. But if an Intruder enters the Landing Zone, you get kicked out.

1. Mission Setup in 5 Simple Steps

A. The Facility Board

Assemble Section borders (A, B, C) and Round track. Place the Landing Zone in Section A with initial supplies (4 Ammo, 4 Grenade, 4 O₂, 4 Medpack). Shuffle Room stacks (A, B, C, and ?) face-down. Connect 3 initial Corridor tiles to the Landing Zone.

B. Systems Status at Game Start

  • Life Support: All 3 tokens start Inactive-side up.
  • Hibernatorium: Starts Inactive-side up and Undiscovered.
  • Anti-Aircraft: Shuffle 2 AA tokens (1 Active, 1 Inactive) face-down in Section B.
  • Nest: Place all 5 Egg tokens on Section C's Eggs track.
  • Robot: Place face-down Robot card in Section A and Robot miniature in Hibernatorium.

C. Operative Draft & Equipment

  1. Deal 1 Help Card per player (sets player number & starting draft order).
  2. Player 1 drafts first: draw 2 Character Draft cards, pick 1, return the other.
  3. Take Character board, set Health to slot 1 (Healthy), set Oxygen counter to 7.
  4. Take starting Character weapon with full Ammo tokens.
  5. Support Equipment Draft: Reveal 7 Support Equipment cards. In reverse player order (Player 5 down to 1), pick 1 card. Fill its gear slots. (The Contractor skips this draft because they already start with 2 items.).
  6. Select 4 starting Tactical Gear tokens of your choice into your Tactical Belt.

D. Objectives

Deal each player 1 Mission Objective and 1 Private Objective face-down. Draw 1 face-up Mission Task on the Round track.

2. Turn & Round Flow

A game lasts up to 14 Rounds. Each round has three distinct phases:

Phase 1: Player Phase

Players act in clockwise order starting from First Player. On your turn, you take 2 Actions. You may:

  • Perform 2 Basic Actions or played Action cards; OR
  • Perform 1 Action and then Pass; OR
  • Pass immediately.

After your turn, the next clockwise player goes. Play continues looping around until every player has Passed.

Objective Selection: Once per game during your turn (before or after an action), you may discard one of your 2 Objectives and keep the other. Advance the Objective Choice track and draw bonus Action cards.

Phase 2: Event Phase

Resolved automatically in strict numerical order:

  1. Escape Check: Any character in the Lander may launch it.
  2. Fire Damage: 1 damage to everything in rooms with Fire markers.
  3. Intruder Attacks: Intruders in rooms with Characters attack (largest first).
  4. Event Card: Draw 1 Event card. Move Intruders by displayed direction icons, then execute card text.
  5. Bag Development: Draw 1 token from the bag and apply the Intruder development table.

Phase 3: Cleanup Phase

  1. Pass First Player token clockwise (skipping dead, escaped, or hibernating players).
  2. Draw Action cards from your deck until you have 5 in hand. (Reshuffle discard if deck is exhausted).
  3. Advance Round marker by 1 space. If entering a space with Autodestruction or Lander arrival, resolve immediately.

3. Character Actions Explained

Paying for Actions

Every Basic Action has a cost in Action Cards (1 or 2). To pay, choose cards from your hand and discard them face-up into your discard pile. Their printed text is ignored when spent as payment. Contamination cards in hand cannot be used to pay action costs.

Movement & Opportunity Attacks

Characters move Room-to-Room along open Corridors. Characters never stop in Corridors.

  • Leaving a Room with Intruders: If you move out of a Room containing Intruders (or through a Corridor containing them), they make an Opportunity Attack before you move. Up to 3 largest Intruders attack.
  • Closed Doors: You cannot move through Closed Doors until opened or destroyed.
  • Noise Roll: After every move, roll the Noise die unless an effect says otherwise.

Move Cautiously

Costs 2 Action Cards. Identical to normal Move, but after entering the destination Room, place 1 Secure Token there immediately. You still make a Noise roll.

Using Rooms

Costs 2 Action Cards. Triggers the unique function of the room you occupy. Cannot be used if the room has a Malfunction Marker (unless an ability explicitly bypasses malfunctions).

Search Action

Search is played via Action Cards (or Room actions). Look at the Item icons on the room tile (Green, Yellow, Red). Draw 1 card from each matching deck, pick at most 1 to keep, and place the rest on the bottom of their decks.

Tactical Gear

Costs 1 Action card. You may activate any number of consumable tokens from your belt:

  • Ammo Token: Full side flips to Half; Half side discards. Reloads a weapon slot.
  • Medpack Token: Restore 2 Health Points.
  • Oxygen Token: Gain +3 Oxygen (max 7).
  • Grenade Token: Target an adjacent Corridor, roll Burst die + 2 hits.

4. Exploration & Doors

Exploration Sequence

When moving into an empty, undiscovered Room slot through an unexplored Corridor:

  1. Draw top card of the Exploration Deck.
  2. Match Room type (A, B, C, or ?). Draw that Room tile and place face-up.
  3. Place new Corridor tiles on indicated unoccupied edges, orienting door slots toward the new room.
  4. Place markers indicated on the card (Malfunction, Fire, Noise, Doors).
  5. Move Character into the room (add Secure token if moving cautiously).
  6. Resolve the card's Entrance Effect (often a Noise roll or placing Adults).
  7. Discard the Exploration card.

Door Rules

Doors can be in three states: Open (no token), Closed (standing token), or Destroyed (laid flat token; permanently open).

  • Blocking: Closed Doors block movement, line-of-sight, shooting, bursting, and fire spread.
  • Noise: Noise markers can be placed across closed doors.
  • Intruder Movement: If an Intruder attempts to move through a Closed Door, it destroys the door instead and stays in place.

5. Combat & Weapons

Shooting (Ranged Weapons)

Costs 1 Action Card. Target an Intruder in your Room.

  1. Weapon must be loaded (at least 1 Ammo token).
  2. Deal 1 Hit (place a Universal marker on target or advance Queen track).
  3. Roll the 8-sided Shoot Die:
    • Skull (Critical): Intruder dies immediately.
    • Number (2–5): Intruder dies if total hits ≥ rolled number.
    • Ammo Loss: Spend 1 Ammo (flip full to half, or discard half). Target survives this hit.
    • Ineffective: Nothing happens; target survives.

KEY FACT: Shooting does NOT spend ammo unless you roll the Ammo-loss die face.

Burst (Suppressive Corridor Fire)

Costs 1 Action Card. Target an adjacent Corridor containing Intruders.

  1. Spend 1 Ammo immediately from your loaded weapon.
  2. Roll the 6-sided Burst Die. Deal that many hits to intruders in the corridor:
    • Larva: 1 hit kills.
    • Adult: 1 hit kills in corridor.
    • Drone: 2 hits kill in corridor.
    • Queen: Each hit advances Queen Hits track by 1.
  3. On the special burst symbol (shared with 4), resolve any extra weapon effect.

Melee Combat

Costs 1 Action card. Highly dangerous. Gain 1 Contamination card. Deal 1 Hit to target in room and roll Shoot die. If the Intruder survives, you must either break one of your weapons (place Malfunction marker) or take an immediate Intruder Attack.

6. Intruders & Noise Mechanics

Intruders come in four castes: Larva, Drone, Adult, and the dreaded Queen.

Corridor vs. Room Durability

  • In Corridors: Adults die in 1 hit. Drones require 2 hits. Intruders never carry lingering damage markers in corridors.
  • In Rooms: Adults and Drones accumulate Hit markers until a Shoot/Melee roll equals or is less than their hit total, or rolls a Skull.

Intruder Movement Rules

  • Intruders engaged in a Room with a Character do not move during normal Event movement.
  • Intruders move along corridors toward the nearest Character.
  • If moving through a Closed Door, they destroy the door and stop.

7. Health, Oxygen & Serious Wounds

Health Track

The health track has three tiers: Healthy, Injured, and Heavily Injured. When taking damage, move marker to the right. Crossing into an Armor slot discards your Armor. Reaching the Skull slot means immediate death.

Oxygen System

Oxygen ranges from 0 to 7. When taking an action or passing in a section with Inactive Life Support, lose 1 O₂. If O₂ reaches the red skull slot, gain a Suffocating Token and reset dial to 0. A suffocating operative dies the next time they lose oxygen.

8. Contamination & Eclosion

Contamination Cards

Drawn into your discard pile when bitten or infected. They clog your deck and cannot be spent to pay action costs.

Infection Procedure

Scan all Contamination cards in hand using the red decoder foil. If any card says INFECTED and you don't already have a Larva on your character board, place 1 Larva model on your board.

Eclosion Procedure (Alien Chest-Burst)

Draw 4 cards from your Action deck into hand without scanning. If your hand contains at least 1 Contamination card: your character dies instantly. An Adult Intruder spawns from your corpse.

9. Life Support & Facility Systems

  • Life Support (A, B, C): Controls atmospheric safety. Inactive sections drain oxygen every turn and make fire spread faster.
  • Anti-Aircraft System: Located in Section B. If Active when the Lander arrives, the Lander is destroyed. Must be inspected/set to Inactive.
  • Autodestruction: Can be armed in Cooling System. Placed 5 rounds ahead on the track. If it detonates, the facility explodes and everyone inside dies.
  • The Reactor: Shuts down facility power completely, deactivating AA, Life Support, and Autodestruction permanently.

10. Escape & Endgame Resolution

The game ends when Round 14 expires, all players escape/die/hibernate, or the facility is destroyed.

Endgame Verification Steps

  1. Resolve Autodestruction if triggered.
  2. Infection Check: Escaped/Hibernated players without a Larva take all cards into hand and resolve Infection Procedure.
  3. Eclosion Check: Escaped/Hibernated players with a Larva gain 1 Contamination, shuffle deck, draw 4 cards for Eclosion. If Contaminated → Death.
  4. Objective Check: Survivors reveal their chosen Objective. If conditions are met → VICTORY.

👾 Intruders & Alien Lifecycle

Know your enemy: stats, spawn rules, damage thresholds, and Queen combat.

LARVA

Health: 1 Hit | Corridor: 1 Hit

Small facehugger parasites. When a Larva attacks an uninfected Character, it burrows onto their Character board as an Infection Larva. In corridors or rooms, 1 hit kills it instantly.

DRONE

Health: Roll-to-Kill | Corridor: 2 Hits

Fast scouting xenomorphs. In rooms, accumulates damage until a Shoot die roll ≤ hits. In corridors, takes 2 Burst hits to kill.

ADULT

Health: Roll-to-Kill | Corridor: 1 Hit

Full-grown deadly predators. In rooms, extremely durable. In corridors, vulnerable to suppressive fire (1 Burst hit kills.). Cause serious wounds and heavy bleed.

👑 THE QUEEN

Health: Queen Health Deck | Armor Track

Massive biomechanical apex predator. Hits are tracked on the Queen Hits track. When the track fills, draw the top Queen Health card, discard bonus cards, and resolve bottom effects. When the deck empties, she dies.

Intruder Attack Resolution

When an Intruder attacks a Character (during the Event Phase, upon room entry, or during opportunity attacks):

  1. Check for Secure Tokens in the room: If the attack is from an Intruder entering, discard 1 Secure token to cancel the attack completely.
  2. If unprevented, draw 1 card from the Intruder Attack Deck.
  3. Find the section matching the attacking caste (Larva, Drone, Adult, Queen).
  4. Apply damage: Health loss, Contamination, or Serious Wounds as printed.
  5. Discard the card.

🎯 Objectives, Escape & Winning

Nemesis is semi-cooperative: the squad has a mission, but private orders may demand sabotage or betrayal.

How Objectives Work

During setup, you receive 1 Mission Objective (aligned with the shared squad goal) and 1 Private Objective (personal orders). At any time during the game on your turn, choose one and banish the other.

Key Rule Terms (Official Glossary)

  • SURVIVOR: A Character alive at the end of round 14 who escaped via Lander/Shuttle OR Hibernated, AND passed the endgame Eclosion test.
  • ESCAPED: Left the facility aboard the Lander or Escape Shuttle (does NOT include Hibernation).
  • FULFILLED: All conditions on your kept Objective card are satisfied when the game concludes.
