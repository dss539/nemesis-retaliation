SOURCE ANCHOR: REPELLING — map-edge block

SOURCE TEXT:
An Intruder moving out of the Unexplored Corridor outside of the visible map. In that case, the Intruder stays where they are.

ASSERTION:
A Repelled Intruder that would leave the visible map through an Unexplored Corridor does not move.

none
