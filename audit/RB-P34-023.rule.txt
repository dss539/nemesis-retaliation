SOURCE ANCHOR: MELEE ATTACK — existing Malfunction

SOURCE TEXT:
Placing a Malfunction marker on a Weapon that already had one destroys the Weapon.

ASSERTION:
If the chosen prevention Weapon already has a Malfunction marker, placing another destroys it.
