RULEBOOK ASSERTION RB-P29-039
KIND: rule
PDF PAGE: 29
PRINTED PAGE: 29
SOURCE ANCHOR: GRENADE LAUNCHER — Burst effect

SOURCE TEXT:
Burst: You may use any number of [Grenade-token glyphs] from this Weapon before or instead of a normal Burst.

ASSERTION:
When Bursting with Grenade Launcher, the Character may use any number of loaded Grenade tokens before or instead of a normal Burst.

VISUAL EVIDENCE:
The purple Grenade-token glyph follows “number of.”

UNCERTAINTY:
none
