SOURCE ANCHOR: MALFUNCTION MARKERS 2. ON HEAVY ITEMS — unusable

SOURCE TEXT:
When a Malfunction marker is placed on a Heavy Item, it cannot be used in any way

ASSERTION:
A Heavy Item with a Malfunction marker cannot be used.
