SOURCE ANCHOR: FIRE MARKERS — Character Turn end

SOURCE TEXT:
When a Character ends their Turn in a Room with a Fire marker, they lose 1 Health point.

ASSERTION:
A Character ending a Turn in a Room with Fire loses 1 Health.

VISUAL EVIDENCE:
A Health glyph follows the amount.

UNCERTAINTY:
none
