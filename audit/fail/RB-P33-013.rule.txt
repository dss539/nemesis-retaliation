RULEBOOK ASSERTION RB-P33-013
KIND: rule
PDF PAGE: 33
PRINTED PAGE: 33
SOURCE ANCHOR: WEAPONS — chosen Weapon effects

SOURCE TEXT:
When a Weapon is chosen to be used in an Action, all effects and modifiers from the chosen Weapon must be applied, unless stated otherwise.

ASSERTION:
Apply every effect and modifier of the Weapon chosen for an Action unless an override says otherwise.

VISUAL EVIDENCE:
none

UNCERTAINTY:
none
