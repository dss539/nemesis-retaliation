Q: Q: Fire At Will – How exactly does that card work?
A: A: The Officer chooses their room or the neighboring one. Then, each Character in a Turn Order Bursts at a Corridor chosen by the Officer. The Officer may choose a different Corridor for each Character to Burst at. Characters, when Bursting, may choose which Weapon to use.
