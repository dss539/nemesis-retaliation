SOURCE ANCHOR: QUEEN ACTIVATION — common cause

SOURCE TEXT:
At many points of the game the Queen may Activate – mostly as a result of drawing a Queen token from the Intruder bag.

ASSERTION:
Drawing a Queen token is the most common Queen-Activation trigger.
