SOURCE ANCHOR: REINFORCED CORRIDOR — Hibernatorium exception

SOURCE TEXT:
Corridors leading to the Hibernatorium can never be Reinforced.

ASSERTION:
A Corridor connected to the Hibernatorium cannot be Reinforced.

none
