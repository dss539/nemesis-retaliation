RULEBOOK ASSERTION RB-P16-030
KIND: rule
PDF PAGE: 16
PRINTED PAGE: 16
SOURCE ANCHOR: GAINING TACTICAL GEAR TOKENS — colored slots

SOURCE TEXT:
Newly gained Tactical Gear tokens can be placed in empty Tactical Gear slots of the corresponding color

ASSERTION:
Place a newly gained Tactical Gear token only in an empty slot of its corresponding color.

VISUAL EVIDENCE:
none

UNCERTAINTY:
The Gray-slot exception is recorded separately.
