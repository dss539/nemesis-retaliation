heading: FACILITY DESTRUCTION
printedDefinition: The Facility can be
Destroyed in a couple of ways during the game.
When it is Destroyed all Characters who have
not Escaped die, all Characters who have Hibernated die, and all Intruders (including the
Queen) die. The Nest is considered Destroyed.
The Facility can be Destroyed as a result of
the Autodestruction Procedure or when a Fire
marker must be placed and there are no more
Fire markers in the pool.
