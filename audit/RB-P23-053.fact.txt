SOURCE ANCHOR: MALFUNCTION MARKERS LIMIT — Weapon example

SOURCE TEXT:
You must place a Malfunction marker on your Weapon and there are no more markers available. You instead place a Fire marker in your Room.

ASSERTION:
If a Weapon must Malfunction with no marker available, its Character’s Room receives Fire instead.
