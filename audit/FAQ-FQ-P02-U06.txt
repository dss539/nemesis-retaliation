Q: Q: An Exploration card tells me to close doors around a newly explored room. Do you only close doors touching the room or all doors in corridors next to the room? What about the hibernatorium doors?
A: A: Only close doors touching the new room. Hibernatorium doors will never close that way, since Exploration is not resolve in the Hibernatorium.
