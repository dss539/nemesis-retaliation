SOURCE ANCHOR: DOORS — Door slots

SOURCE TEXT:
Some Corridors may come with Door slots – this means that there are Open Doors there.

ASSERTION:
A Corridor Door slot represents an Open Door when no token covers it.

VISUAL EVIDENCE:
The diagram labels a Door-slot opening and shows CLOSED/DESTROYED token states.

none
