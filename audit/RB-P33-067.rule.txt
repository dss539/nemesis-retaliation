SOURCE ANCHOR: BURSTING — Queen allocation

SOURCE TEXT:
You may apply any number of Hits to the Queen, up to her Hits track maximum.

ASSERTION:
Allocate any number of Burst Hits to the Queen without exceeding the remaining Queen Hits-track capacity.
