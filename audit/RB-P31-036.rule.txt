SOURCE ANCHOR: RARE MOVEMENT EXCEPTIONS — equal routes

SOURCE TEXT:
two different routes may be the same length ... use the shortest route that starts with the Corridor/Room with the lowest ID number.

ASSERTION:
If shortest routes tie, use the route whose first Corridor or Room has the lowest ID.
