Q: Q: Are effects on Weapons, when rolling a specific result on a die, in addition to a standard result, or instead of it?
A: A: Unless the Weapon instructs you to specifically do something “instead”, the effect described is resolved in addition to any standard effect. For example, when rolling an [FQ-P02-U21-I01] on a Shoot die, you still spend Ammo, even if your Weapon allows you to “[FQ-P02-U21-I02]: resolve [FQ-P02-U21-I03]”.
