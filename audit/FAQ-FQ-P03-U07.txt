Q: Q: Can I heal other players with Restore [Hp] effects (medic’s Actions cards, green tactical token)? What about other effects?
A: A:The interplay rules should apply to all forms of Actions. Action cards, Item cards, Tactical Gear tokens, etc. are all fair deal to use on other Characters, as long as the receiving Character allows you and the effect includes 1 of the 4 mentioned in the rulebook. Other types of Actions and effects which are not Actions cannot be used on other Characters.
