RULEBOOK ASSERTION RB-P22-014
KIND: rule
PDF PAGE: 22
PRINTED PAGE: 22
SOURCE ANCHOR: BLOCKING PATH — Character traversal

SOURCE TEXT:
Characters cannot Move, Burst etc. through a Corridor with Closed Doors

ASSERTION:
A Character cannot Move, Burst, or otherwise act through a Corridor blocked by a Closed Door.

VISUAL EVIDENCE:
A Character glyph follows “Characters.”

UNCERTAINTY:
none
