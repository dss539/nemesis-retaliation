Q: Q: Can I do (...) through Closed Doors? What exactly are Objects?
A: A: Objects include Doors, Character, Intruders, basically everything excluding Noise markers. Through doors you cannot Reinforce a Corridor, Command another Character, Burst, throw Grenades, etc. You can place and discard Noise markers.
