SOURCE ANCHOR: ARMOR ITEMS — break trigger

SOURCE TEXT:
When a Health marker is to be moved to a Health track section with an Armor Item, that Armor Item breaks and must be discarded

ASSERTION:
Discard Armor when the Health marker would enter the section it covers.
