SOURCE ANCHOR: ROBOT MOVEMENT — Unexplored Corridors

SOURCE TEXT:
The Robot may never move through an Unexplored Corridor to Explore a new Room

ASSERTION:
A normal Robot cannot Explore through an Unexplored Corridor.
