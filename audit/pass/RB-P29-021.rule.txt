SOURCE ANCHOR: ARMOR ITEMS — gain while Heavily Injured

SOURCE TEXT:
When a Character gains an Armor Item and their Health marker is already on the Heavily Injured section, they can’t gain the Armor

ASSERTION:
A Character whose Health marker is already Heavily Injured cannot gain Armor.

none

UNCERTAINTY:
none
