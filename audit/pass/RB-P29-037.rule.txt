SOURCE ANCHOR: TACTICAL GEAR SLOTS — lost Item

SOURCE TEXT:
As a result of losing an Item, the Character also loses all Tactical Gear tokens from that Item.

ASSERTION:
When an Item is lost, also lose every Tactical Gear token on it.
