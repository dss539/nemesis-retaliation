SOURCE ANCHOR: INTRUDER LIMITS — placing displacement

SOURCE TEXT:
When placing, remove the smaller Intruders from the Corridor as neccessary to place larger Intruders in their place.

ASSERTION:
When placing a larger Intruder into a full Corridor, remove as many smaller Intruders as necessary to make room.

VISUAL EVIDENCE:
none

UNCERTAINTY:
The source misspells “necessary” as “neccessary.”
