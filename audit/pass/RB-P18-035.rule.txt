KIND: rule
PDF PAGE: 18
PRINTED PAGE: 18
SOURCE ANCHOR: SERIOUS WOUNDS — occupied-section displacement

SOURCE TEXT:
If the Health marker is in a section where a Serious Wound card is being placed, move the Health marker to the first empty slot in the next Section.

ASSERTION:
When a Serious Wound is placed in the marker’s current Health section, move the marker to the first empty slot in the next section.

VISUAL EVIDENCE:
none

UNCERTAINTY:
none
