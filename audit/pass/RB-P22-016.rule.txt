SOURCE ANCHOR: BLOCKING PATH — Intruders

SOURCE TEXT:
Intruders, when attempting to move through Closed Doors, Destroy them instead.

ASSERTION:
An Intruder attempting to cross a Closed Door destroys it instead of moving through.

VISUAL EVIDENCE:
An Intruder glyph follows “Intruders.”

none
