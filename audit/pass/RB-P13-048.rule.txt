SOURCE ANCHOR: COMMANDS RESTRICTIONS — Opportunity Attack

You are never allowed to perform an Action using another Character that would result in an Intruder Opportunity Attack

ASSERTION:
An ordered Action cannot cause an Intruder Opportunity Attack.
