RULEBOOK ASSERTION RB-P12-043
KIND: rule
PDF PAGE: 12
PRINTED PAGE: 12
SOURCE ANCHOR: NOT IN COMBAT — restriction

SOURCE TEXT:
Some Actions (marked with [Not In Combat glyph]) may only be performed, when a Character is not engaged in Combat.

ASSERTION:
An Action marked Not In Combat may be performed only when the Character is not in Combat.

VISUAL EVIDENCE:
The red crossed-Intruder glyph shown in the callout is the restriction marker.

UNCERTAINTY:
none
