KIND: rule

SOURCE ANCHOR: ESCAPING/HIBERNATING — failed Noise

SOURCE TEXT:
If, after this Noise roll, there is an Intruder in the same Room as the Character, the Action is unsuccessful

ASSERTION:
The exit/Hibernation attempt fails if its Noise roll leaves an Intruder in the Character’s Room.

VISUAL EVIDENCE:
An Intruder glyph appears in the condition.

UNCERTAINTY:
none
