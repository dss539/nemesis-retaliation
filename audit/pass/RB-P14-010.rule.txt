SOURCE ANCHOR: 2. INTRUDER ATTACKS — Room order

SOURCE TEXT:
Each Attack is resolved separately starting with the Room on the top-left of the Facility and going row by row.

ASSERTION:
Resolve Intruder Attacks separately by Rooms, starting at the Facility’s top-left and proceeding row by row.
