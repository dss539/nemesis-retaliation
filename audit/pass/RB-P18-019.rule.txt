SOURCE ANCHOR: CHARACTER’S HEALTH AND WOUNDS — death threshold

SOURCE TEXT:
When a Health marker is moved from the last slot onto the Skull icon, the Character dies.

ASSERTION:
A Character dies when their Health marker moves beyond the final Heavily Injured slot onto the Skull.

VISUAL EVIDENCE:
The Skull icon is printed immediately after the final red slot.

UNCERTAINTY:
none
