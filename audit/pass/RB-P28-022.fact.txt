SOURCE ANCHOR: ITEM TRAITS — Requires no Ammo qualification

SOURCE TEXT:
For the sake of Actions that require Weapons with Ammo tokens, those Items are considered as always having an Ammo token on them.

ASSERTION:
A Requires No Ammo Weapon counts as having an Ammo token for Action prerequisites.
