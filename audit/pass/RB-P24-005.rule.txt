SOURCE ANCHOR: MOVEMENT SEQUENCE — Opportunity Attack condition

SOURCE TEXT:
If there are any Intruders in the same Room as the Character

ASSERTION:
Each Intruder in the Character’s starting Room makes an Opportunity Attack during Movement.

VISUAL EVIDENCE:
An Intruder glyph appears in the condition.

UNCERTAINTY:
Subject to the three-Attack maximum.
