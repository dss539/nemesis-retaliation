SOURCE ANCHOR: LANDER TOKEN — successful branch condition

SOURCE TEXT:
If it is Inactive (or Anti-Aircraft tokens have been removed from the game), the Lander lands succesfully

ASSERTION:
The Lander lands successfully if the revealed Anti-Aircraft token is Inactive or no Anti-Aircraft tokens remain in the game.

VISUAL EVIDENCE:
The green INACTIVE face is printed beside this branch.

UNCERTAINTY:
The source spells “succesfully” with one s after succe.
