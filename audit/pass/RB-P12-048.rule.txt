SOURCE ANCHOR: COST IN ACTION CARDS — no effects

SOURCE TEXT:
Action cards discarded ... as a result of any effect are placed on a discard pile without resolving their effects.

ASSERTION:
Do not resolve an Action card’s effect when another effect discards it.
