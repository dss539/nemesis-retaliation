KIND: rule
PDF PAGE: 30

SOURCE ANCHOR: INTRUDER MOVEMENT — occupied Room entry

SOURCE TEXT:
Whenever Intruders move from 1 Corridor to a Room with a Character, only the largest Intruder moves to that Room instead.

ASSERTION:
When a group would enter a Character’s Room from a Corridor, move only the group’s largest Intruder.

none

UNCERTAINTY:
none
