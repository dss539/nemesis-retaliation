SOURCE ANCHOR: DISCARDING SERIOUS WOUNDS — choice

SOURCE TEXT:
Whenever a Character resolves an effect that discards a Serious Wound and they have more than one, they may choose which one to discard.

ASSERTION:
If an effect discards one Serious Wound from a Character with several, that Character chooses which one.
