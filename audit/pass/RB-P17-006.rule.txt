RULEBOOK ASSERTION RB-P17-006
KIND: rule
PDF PAGE: 17
PRINTED PAGE: 17
SOURCE ANCHOR: E. OXYGEN — spending floor

SOURCE TEXT:
You are unable to use Actions that require you to spend Oxygen if you would drop below 0 as a result.

ASSERTION:
A Character cannot perform an Action whose Oxygen cost would reduce their Oxygen below 0.

none
