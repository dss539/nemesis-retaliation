SOURCE ANCHOR: INTRUDERS AND NOISE MARKERS — prohibition

SOURCE TEXT:
Noise markers are never placed in Corridors containing Intruders.

ASSERTION:
Do not place Noise markers in an Intruder-occupied Corridor.
