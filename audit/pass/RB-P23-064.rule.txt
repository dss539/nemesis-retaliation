SOURCE ANCHOR: RESOLVING SECURE TOKENS — resident Intruders

SOURCE TEXT:
Secure tokens do NOT protect from Intruder Attacks made by Intruders that are already in a Room

ASSERTION:
A Secure token does not prevent Attacks by Intruders already in its Room.
