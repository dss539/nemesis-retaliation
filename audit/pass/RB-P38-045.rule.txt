SOURCE ANCHOR: ESCAPING/HIBERNATING — Noise

SOURCE TEXT:
Whenever a Character tries to use one of the 3 aforementioned Rooms, they must make a Noise roll as a part of that Room’s effect.

ASSERTION:
An attempt to board the Lander, Hibernate, or use the Escape Shuttle includes a Noise roll.
