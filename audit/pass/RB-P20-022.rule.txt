RULEBOOK ASSERTION RB-P20-022
KIND: rule
PDF PAGE: 20
PRINTED PAGE: 20
SOURCE ANCHOR: Room special icon — no Malfunction marker

SOURCE TEXT:
A Malfunction marker is never placed on them.

ASSERTION:
A Room with the crossed-Malfunction special icon cannot receive a Malfunction marker.

VISUAL EVIDENCE:
The rule is preceded by a red crossed-gear glyph.

UNCERTAINTY:
none
