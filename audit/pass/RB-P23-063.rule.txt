SOURCE ANCHOR: RESOLVING SECURE TOKENS — prevention

SOURCE TEXT:
Discarding Secure tokens is a result of Intruder movement and may never be prevented.

ASSERTION:
No effect can prevent a Secure token from being discarded due to Intruder movement.

none
