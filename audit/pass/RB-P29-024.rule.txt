SOURCE ANCHOR: HEAVY ARMOR — replacement effect

SOURCE TEXT:
Whenever you would gain a Serious Wound, you may lose 2 [Health] instead.

ASSERTION:
Heavy Armor may replace gaining a Serious Wound with losing 2 Health.

VISUAL EVIDENCE:
A heartbeat Health glyph follows the number 2.

UNCERTAINTY:
none
