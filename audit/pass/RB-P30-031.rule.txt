SOURCE ANCHOR: INTRUDER MOVEMENT — Corridor origin

SOURCE TEXT:
If the moving Intruders are in a Corridor, they move to an adjacent Room.

ASSERTION:
An Intruder moving from a Corridor enters an adjacent Room.

none
