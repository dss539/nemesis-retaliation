SOURCE ANCHOR: TURN ORDER AS TIEBREAKER — definition

SOURCE TEXT:
In any such case it describes the Starting player ... or is closest to one in the clockwise Turn order.

ASSERTION:
“First in Turn order” means the Starting Player or the Character closest clockwise after the Starting Player.

VISUAL EVIDENCE:
none

UNCERTAINTY:
The source grammar omits “the Character who” before “is closest.”
