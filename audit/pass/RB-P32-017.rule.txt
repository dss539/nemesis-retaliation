SOURCE ANCHOR: INTRUDERS CHOOSING A TARGET — player-effect target

SOURCE TEXT:
If the Intruder entered as a result of a player effect, they Attack that Character if possible.

ASSERTION:
When player-caused entry triggers the Attack, target the Character who caused entry if possible.
