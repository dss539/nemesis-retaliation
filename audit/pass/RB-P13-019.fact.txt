SOURCE ANCHOR: REACTIONS — definition

SOURCE TEXT:
Some Action cards may be used outside of normal Turn order to react to something happening in the game – they are Reactions.

ASSERTION:
A Reaction is an Action-card use outside normal Turn order in response to a game event.
