RULEBOOK ASSERTION RB-P12-024
KIND: rule
PDF PAGE: 12
PRINTED PAGE: 12
SOURCE ANCHOR: PLAYERS’ TURNS — ordered sequence

SOURCE TEXT:
During their Turn a player must resolve the 3 following steps in order: 1. Perform 2 Actions 2. Oxygen Loss 3. Fire Damage

ASSERTION:
Resolve Perform 2 Actions, Oxygen Loss, and Fire Damage in that order on every Turn.

VISUAL EVIDENCE:
The numbered vertical list fixes the sequence.

UNCERTAINTY:
none
