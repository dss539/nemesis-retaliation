SOURCE ANCHOR: A. SECTIONS SETUP — Ammo-token note

SOURCE TEXT:
All Ammo tokens enter the game (during setup or during the game) on their full side.

ASSERTION:
Whenever an Ammo token enters the game, it enters full-side-up.

VISUAL EVIDENCE:
The note pictures the full and half-full faces.

UNCERTAINTY:
none
