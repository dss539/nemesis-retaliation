SOURCE ANCHOR: GOLDEN RULES — Local Effects example

SOURCE TEXT:
“Discard a Malfunction marker” means you discard a Malfunction marker from the Room your Character is currently in or any object in your Room

ASSERTION:
An unspecified “Discard a Malfunction marker” effect can remove one from the Character’s Room or an object in that Room.
