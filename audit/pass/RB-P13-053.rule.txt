SOURCE ANCHOR: EFFECTS — Duck and Cover example

SOURCE TEXT:
If you don’t have the required number of additional Action cards in hand you can’t play “Duck and Cover.”

ASSERTION:
A player cannot play Duck and Cover without the required additional Action card in hand.
