SOURCE ANCHOR: DOORS — placement orientation

SOURCE TEXT:
All Corridors, at the moment of placement, should be oriented with a Door slot in the direction of the Room just placed.

ASSERTION:
When placing a Corridor with a Door slot, orient that slot toward the newly placed Room.

VISUAL EVIDENCE:
The example diagram shows slot ends facing their new Rooms.

UNCERTAINTY:
none
