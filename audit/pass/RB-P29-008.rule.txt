SOURCE ANCHOR: HEAVY ITEMS — gain with full Hands

SOURCE TEXT:
If such an Item is gained when all Hand slots are already taken, you may first discard one of the held Items to make space for the new Item.

ASSERTION:
When gaining a Heavy Item with no empty Hand slot, the Character may discard one held Item first to make room.
