SOURCE ANCHOR: TACTICAL GEAR SLOTS — gained token

SOURCE TEXT:
When gaining a Tactical Gear token, it may be placed in any your empty Tactical Gear slot that matches the token

ASSERTION:
A gained Tactical Gear token may be placed in any empty matching slot the Character owns.
