KIND: rule

SOURCE ANCHOR: COST IN ACTION CARDS — no effects

SOURCE TEXT:
Action cards discarded to pay a cost of Basic Actions ... are placed on a discard pile without resolving their effects.

ASSERTION:
Do not resolve an Action card’s effect when discarding it to pay a Basic Action cost.
