SOURCE ANCHOR: ARMOR ITEMS — placement

SOURCE TEXT:
When gained, an Armor Item is always placed on the Heavily Injured section of the Health track.

ASSERTION:
Place a gained Armor Item over the Heavily Injured Health section.
