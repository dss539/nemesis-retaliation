SOURCE ANCHOR: INTRUDERS AND NOISE MARKERS

SOURCE TEXT:
When an Intruder is placed or moved to a Corridor with a Noise marker, discard the Noise marker first.

ASSERTION:
Before placing or moving an Intruder into a Corridor containing Noise, discard that Noise marker.
