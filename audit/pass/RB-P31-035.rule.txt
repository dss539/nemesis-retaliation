RULEBOOK ASSERTION RB-P31-035
KIND: rule
PDF PAGE: 31
PRINTED PAGE: 31
SOURCE ANCHOR: INTRUDERS MOVING THROUGH DOORS — collision

SOURCE TEXT:
Whenever Intruders would move through a Closed Door, they destroy that Door and do not move instead.

ASSERTION:
An Intruder that would cross a Closed Door destroys it and stops instead.

none
