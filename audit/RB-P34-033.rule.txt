SOURCE ANCHOR: INTRUDER HIT MARKERS — movement cleanup

SOURCE TEXT:
An injured Intruder moving to a Corridor discards all Hit markers they may have had.

ASSERTION:
Remove every Hit marker from an injured Intruder when it moves into a Corridor.

none
