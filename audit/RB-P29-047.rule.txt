SOURCE ANCHOR: INTERPLAY — Malfunction

SOURCE TEXT:
Discarding [Malfunction glyph] (from Items belonging to another Character)

ASSERTION:
An Item may be used on another consenting Character in the same Room to discard a Malfunction marker from that Character’s Item.

VISUAL EVIDENCE:
A gear-shaped Malfunction glyph follows “Discarding.”

none
