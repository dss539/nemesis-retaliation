SOURCE ANCHOR: MALFUNCTION MARKERS 1. ON ROOMS — Room Action

SOURCE TEXT:
If a Room has a Malfunction marker its Room Action is unavailable

ASSERTION:
A Room with a Malfunction marker has no available Room Action.
