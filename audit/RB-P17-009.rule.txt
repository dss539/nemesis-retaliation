RULEBOOK ASSERTION RB-P17-009
KIND: rule
PDF PAGE: 17
PRINTED PAGE: 17
SOURCE ANCHOR: SPENDING OXYGEN

SOURCE TEXT:
Some Items or effects may instruct you to spend Oxygen by rotating your Oxygen dial an appropriate number of times.

ASSERTION:
When an Item or effect spends Oxygen, rotate the Oxygen dial by the specified amount.
