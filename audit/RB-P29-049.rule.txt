RULEBOOK ASSERTION RB-P29-049
KIND: rule
PDF PAGE: 29
PRINTED PAGE: 29
SOURCE ANCHOR: INTERPLAY — other effects

SOURCE TEXT:
For any other effect, you must Trade the Item to another Character, so that they can use it themselves.

ASSERTION:
For an unlisted effect, Trade the Item to the other Character before that Character uses it.
