SOURCE ANCHOR: ARMOR ITEMS — replacement

SOURCE TEXT:
When a Character gains an Armor Item while already using one, they may first discard the Armor from their Health track to gain the new Armor.

ASSERTION:
A Character may discard worn Armor before gaining replacement Armor.
