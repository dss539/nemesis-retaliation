Q: How does shooting at the Queen work exactly?
A: Shooting at the Queen works exactly the same as it does for Adult Intruders, but instead of removing the miniature from the board, you resolve the Queen health card instead. Shooting a Queen with 2 hits will result in resolving the top card of the Queen Health Deck, if a player rolls a 2 (or fewer) or a skull symbol.
