Q: Q: Shelter – can Items “discard” Secure tokens from this room for their effects?
A: A: No. The Shelter “always secured” should be treated as a permanent status of the room (preventing Attacks from incoming Intruders), not as a Secure token. The “always secured” is not a Secure token, and as such cannot be discarded by Items (or for example, counted for Sharpshooter’s “Hold Position” card). Additional Secure tokens may be placed in this room with a standard limit of 3. Those tokens are discarded first in case of any effects (Intruders entering the room, items, etc.).
