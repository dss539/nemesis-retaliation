SOURCE ANCHOR: MOVEMENT SEQUENCE — unexplored destination

SOURCE TEXT:
If you are Moving through an Unexplored Corridor to an Undiscovered Room – Resolve the Exploration Sequence.

ASSERTION:
Movement through an Unexplored Corridor to an Undiscovered Room resolves the Exploration Sequence instead of the discovered-destination branch.
