SOURCE ANCHOR: SPEND Ammo — full token

SOURCE TEXT:
If you have a full Ammo token and the game instructs you to spend it – flip it.

ASSERTION:
To spend full Ammo, flip the token to half-full.

VISUAL EVIDENCE:
The SPEND diagram shows the full token turning over.

UNCERTAINTY:
none
