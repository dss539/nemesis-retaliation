  Note: The rulebook uses "empty Room slot" or "Room slot" for positions
    where a Room tile may be placed. The codebase pervasively calls these
    "nodes" (from graph theory), and "empty nodes" or "empty space."
  Suggested: Replace "node" with "roomSlot" in identifiers and "Room slot"
    in comments/UI strings. Replace "empty node" with "empty Room slot."

  Canonical: "Facility" (the overall map/board is called the Facility)
  Note: The state property `mapGrid` stores the spatial index of rooms and
    corridors. The rulebook calls the entire playing surface "the Facility"
    or "the Map." There is no single "mapGrid" concept — rooms and corridors
    occupy slots within the Facility.
  Suggested: Rename to `facilityGrid` or `boardLayout`. The concept it
    represents (spatial index of placed tiles) has no direct rulebook
    analogue — UNRESOLVED whether a rename is strictly necessary since it
    is an internal data structure, but `facilityGrid` better aligns with
    rulebook language.

  Location: Throughout engine.js, ui.js, network.js, main.js
  Canonical: "Character" — the rulebook consistently uses "Character"
    for player miniatures/standees and "player" for the real-world
    person playing the game.
  Note: This is the single most pervasive divergence. The codebase
    uses "player" for both the real-world person AND the in-game
    Character, while the rulebook distinguishes them. Player-facing
    text (UI labels, log messages) should use "Character" where
    referring to the in-game entity.
  Examples of divergence:

      player's display name is separate from the Character type)

      "Character moves to" per rulebook style
    - UI "player-boards" should be "character-boards"
  Suggested: UNRESOLVED — a full rename would be extensive. Prioritize
    player-facing strings (UI labels, log messages) to use "Character"
    where the rulebook would. Internal identifiers can remain "player"
    as a pragmatic choice.
