In dire situations, a Character ( ) may choose to Melee Attack
an Intruder ( ) in the same Room. This should be done only as
a last resort, because if the Intruder lives through the Attack it will
try to retaliate.

larva health
In a Corridor 1 Hit is enough to kill a Larva.
In a Room 1 Hit is also enough to kill a Larva – regardless of the number rolled on a Shoot die.

drone health
Killing Drones is only harder when they are in Corridors (2 Hits are
needed to kill them).
Shooting them in Rooms is always resolved the same as Shooting
Adult Intruders.
INTRUDER HIT MARKERS
Intruders in Corridors never have left-over Hit markers placed
beside them.
An injured Intruder moving to a Corridor discards all Hit markers they may have had.
