Rulebook lines 2871–2875:
Costing 0 Action cards:
• Play an Action card
• Pass

Rulebook lines 3177–3179:
When the “Play an Action card” Action is performed, the player reveals the chosen Action card from their hand, resolves its effect, and then places it on top of the discard pile.

Rulebook lines 3188–3191:
When choosing an effect (from Action card, Item card, Rooms etc.) You must be able to resolve the effect entirely. If it’s impossible, you may not choose that effect.

Rulebook lines 3543–3548:
Component Limits – Most components are limited and have special rules on what happens if they run out. For any other components, consider them limited with no special rules – if they are to be used, and no more are available, nothing happens instead. For example: Characters cannot gain Ammo tokens if all are already in players’ possession.

Rulebook lines 4849–4865:
Items are most commonly gained by using the Search Action card. The Search Action card allows you to draw 1 Item card for each Item Icon (green, yellow, and red) on the Room you are Searching in. Then, you pick 1 of those cards to keep and discard the rest to the bottom of their respective decks. Items that were Searched but not chosen should not be revealed to other players. Example: searching a Room with one green and one red icon draws one green and one red Item, keeps one, and puts the other on the bottom of its corresponding deck.

Rulebook lines 4899–4904:
During the game Items are always found with all Tactical Gear slots filled, if they have any.

Rulebook lines 4905–4910:
All regular Items are always placed in a Backpack card holder. There is no limit to the number of regular Items that may be held in the Backpack. Items in the Backpack are secret information and should not be revealed to other players until used.

Rulebook lines 4933–4948:
Most Items come with Traits below their name. Heavy: horizontal Items placed only in Hand slots. Armor: Items marked Armor. One Use Only: discarded when Used. Ranged Weapon and Melee Weapon are descriptive traits. Requires no Ammo has its printed meaning.

Rulebook lines 5024–5034:
All horizontal Items are Heavy. Heavy Items do not fit in a Backpack and must be held in a Hand slot. Only 1 Item may be carried in a Hand slot. If a Heavy Item is gained when all Hand slots are occupied, the Character may first discard one held Item to make space. Weapons and Intruder Eggs are Heavy.

Rulebook lines 5045–5057:
Armor Items have the Armor keyword and are placed on the Heavily Injured Health-track section. Only one Armor may be worn. When gaining Armor while already using one, the Character may first discard the old Armor to gain the new one. If the Health marker is already on Heavily Injured, the Character cannot gain the Armor and it is discarded instead.

EXACT SEARCH ACTION-CARD FACE
For each Item Icon in your Room draw 1 Item of the corresponding type. You may keep 1 of them and discard the rest.
Restriction glyph: Not In Combat.
