# Nemesis: Retaliation — Complete Card Reference

Compiled from: `js/data.js` (repo game data), official rulebook PDF text, BGG card-list thread (#3558901), and Order of Gamers rules summary v1.2.

---

## 1. CHARACTER ACTION CARDS (6 characters × 10 cards = 60)

Each character has a unique 10-card action deck. Cards are played to perform actions or pay action costs. Some cards have Reaction effects.

### Characters

| Character | Rank | Health | Start Item | Special Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Contractor | 3 | 9 | None (no support equipment) | — (versatile, more flexibility) |
| Recon | 1 | 9 | Recon Scanner | Sprint (move, then may spend 1 to move again) |
| Officer | 4 | 9 | Officer Pistol | Command (order lower-rank characters) |
| Medical Support | 2 | 9 | Field Medkit | Rest (draw cards, perform infection check) |
| Heavy Gun Operator | 2 | 9 | Automatic Shotgun | — (bonus hit on shooting) |
| Combat Engineer | 2 | 9 | Tactical Hatchet | Reinforce, Drill (new corridors) |

### Character-Specific Action/Reaction Cards (from rulebook)

**Recon — Sprint**: Move. Then, you may spend 1 [action card] to Move again.

**Contractor: Consultant — Duck and Cover**: Discard 1 [action card] to Move. During that Movement, Prevent 1 Intruder Attack. When you would be Attacked by an [Intruder] in a Room with another [Character]: The [Intruder] Attacks the other [Character] instead.

**Heavy Gun Operator — Automatic Shotgun (Character Item / Support Equipment)**: Shoot: Before Shooting, deal 1 Hit more. Burst: Spend 1 [Ammo] if able. (RANGED WEAPON, HEAVY)

**Combat Engineer — Tactical Hatchet (Character Item / Support Equipment)**: Melee Attack: Deal 1 Hit instead of rolling the Shoot die, and place a [Malfunction] on this Weapon. (MELEE WEAPON, HEAVY)

**Recon — Sonic Gun (Character Item)**: Burst: Treat 3 and 4 as 2 [Hits]. Repel: Place 1 [Intruder] in your Room back to Corridor. (RANGED WEAPON, REQUIRES NO AMMO, HEAVY)

**Contractor: Consultant — Weak Spot** (Serious Wound card): Deal 1 Hit to an [Intruder]. Then, Shoot or Melee Attack it.

**EYES** (Serious Wound card): +1 to all your Shoot results.

## 6. CONTAMINATION CARDS (27 cards, ~7 Infected)

All contamination cards have the same text. Some are secretly INFECTED.

**Front text**: Contamination — Can't be discarded for Actions. You may discard this card when you Pass.

**Infected cards**: ~7 of the 27 are marked INFECTED on the back (revealed when scanned or at game end). If a character has an Infected card at game end and a Larva is on their character board, they die.

## 13. CORRIDOR TILES (40 tiles: 10 each of values 1-4)

Not cards per se, but tiles drawn during exploration. Value 0 side is the "reinforced" side. Doors are placed on slots connecting to the newly explored room.

## CARD ART AVAILABILITY

The digital edition (this repo) currently uses text-based cards — no card art is implemented.

### Where to find official card art:

1. **BGG Image Gallery** — `boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/381248/nemesis-retaliation/images` — Has user-uploaded photos of card art, but currently Cloudflare-blocked for automated access. Users can browse manually.

2. **BGG "Card Altar" blog post** — `boardgamegeek.com/blog/16351/blogpost/184135` — A dedicated blog post with close-up card photography. Also Cloudflare-blocked.

3. **YouTube unboxing videos** — Several unboxing videos show card art close-ups:
   - "Nemesis Retaliation | Unboxing the Core Game, Stretch Goals & Untold Stories Vol 4" (BGG video 571650)
   - "Nemesis Retaliation l All in Unboxing" (YouTube)
   - "Nemesis: Retaliation Unboxing: Stretch Goals" (BGG video 589291) — Shows Neoflesh Cult cards at timestamp 18:41, character cards at 19:49

4. **Tabletop Simulator mod** — Steam Workshop ID 3256296893 — Contains digitized card images for all base game cards. Accessible via TTS.

5. **Awaken Realms downloads** — `awakenrealms.com/download` → Nemesis Retaliation section — Has rulebooks, FAQ, and sheets but no card art scans.

6. **Facebook unboxing group** — "Nemesis: Retaliation unboxing with close-up artwork and miniatures" — User post focusing on card artwork close-ups.
